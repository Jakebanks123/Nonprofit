Unlock Gov

Find the UK and local council support you may be able to get.

This is a working prototype, not a live service. Figures and thresholds below are illustrative approximations, not verified current rates. Local council schemes cover 12 pilot councils to test the concept. Always confirm details on GOV.UK or your council's website before relying on any figure here — this tool is not financial or legal advice. Nothing you type is stored, and the only thing ever sent anywhere is your postcode, if you use the lookup — sent to the free public postcodes.io API purely to identify your council.
Illustrative rates approx. 2024/25, for demonstration only — verify at gov.uk/browse/benefits before making any decisions. Built as a prototype nonprofit tool to help people find support they may be entitled to.
